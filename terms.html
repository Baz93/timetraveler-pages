TimeTraveler — rules

TimeTraveler lets players share levels, collections and their names with everyone. By making an account you agree to these rules.

What you share

  • Nothing hateful, harassing or offensive — about anyone, or any group of people.
  • Nothing sexual or violent.
  • No spam, advertising or scams; no one else's personal details.
  • Nothing that breaks the law or someone else's rights.

This goes for level and collection names, and for your own name.

Reports

Anyone can report a level, a collection or a player from its page in the game (Report). Reports are looked at within 24 hours. What breaks the rules is renamed or removed, and an account that keeps breaking them is closed. You can also block a player: what they share leaves your lists.

What stays

A published level is everyone's to play and keep in their collections, so it isn't taken back: you can hide it (your name goes from it), and if you delete your account, your levels stay without your name.

No warranty

The game is provided as it is. It may change, and the online part may be unavailable at times; what you play offline stays on your device.

See also the privacy policy. Questions: game.timetraveler@proton.me.

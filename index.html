TimeTraveler — privacy policy

TimeTraveler is a puzzle game. It can be played with no account: then everything stays on your device, and nothing about you is sent anywhere.

With an account, the game's server keeps

  • your login and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself);
  • your name, which everyone sees;
  • what you play and make: your runs of levels, the levels and collections you publish, your drafts (seen by you only), the levels and collections you open, your likes, the players you follow or block, and your reports;
  • a token for each device you're signed in on.

Everyone can see your name, what you publish, your records and your place on leaderboards. Your login, drafts, blocks and reports are seen by no one else.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of your data with anyone. The game asks for no permissions on your device beyond the internet. The connection to the server is encrypted (HTTPS).

Deleting your account

In the game: Profile → Delete account. Or on the web: delete your account. It deletes at once your login, name, runs, likes, follows, blocks, drafts, collections and reports. Levels you published stay in the game without your name.

Children

The game isn't made for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly keep their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will say so. Questions or requests: game.timetraveler@proton.me.

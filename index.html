TimeTraveler — support

TimeTraveler is a grid puzzle about pushing boxes and travelling back in time: summon your future self to help, then go back in time to become them.

Contact

Questions, bugs, ideas, a level that's wrong, a forgotten password: game.timetraveler@proton.me. We answer within a few days.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No — the whole game plays on your device. An account keeps your progress on every device, and lets you publish levels and set records.
  • Where's my progress after signing in? What you played before signing in goes to the account you sign in to first.
  • Something offensive in a level or a name? Report it from its page (Report), or block its author.
